Two traders take the same trade. One lets the stop close it. The other drags the stop lower.

Nothing about the second trade got better. Same instrument, same second, same idea — and the idea was wrong on both sides. A stop is the price where that gets proven. Move it and the idea is still wrong; you have only removed the number that was measuring it.

The first trader knew the cost before the entry. The second one finds out after, and by then it is not a decision anymore.

A stop tightens. It does not retreat.
