Comment "MAP" for the free beta of my indicator. The retest isn't owed to you — and the comfort you're waiting for is information about the move.

A retest happens when there are still participants willing to trade back at that level. When a move is strong enough that nobody is, price just keeps going. So the entries that feel safest follow the least convincing moves — and insisting on comfort quietly selects for the moves least likely to run.

THE LINE — decide in advance which one you're trading: the move, or the comfort.
