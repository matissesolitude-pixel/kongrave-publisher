The first goes down through the timeframes until something supports the idea he arrived with, and on the fourth one it finally does. The second looks at one timeframe he decided on before opening the chart, it says nothing, and he closes it. Run it again with the opposite intention and the same chart hands the first trader three reasons to sell.

A reason you had to hunt for is a reason the market never gave you.
