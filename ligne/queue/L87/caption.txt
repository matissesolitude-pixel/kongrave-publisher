Two accounts take the same drawdown, on the same instrument, in the same session. One keeps trading. The other gets closed by the broker.

It isn't the loss and it isn't the direction — both were wrong in exactly the same way at the same moment. What differed was underneath: how much cushion was left holding the position open. One was using a fraction of the available leverage, the other almost all of it, so the same move ate a different share of what kept the account alive.
