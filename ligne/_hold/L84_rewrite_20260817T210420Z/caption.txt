Two plans are built on the same year of data. One has four rules, the other has fourteen — and on that year, fourteen looks flawless.

Almost every loss has been ruled out by a condition. But each condition was added after seeing the loss it removes: the plan didn't predict the year, it memorised it. Run both on a year neither has seen and the simple plan performs about the same, while the perfect one falls apart.
