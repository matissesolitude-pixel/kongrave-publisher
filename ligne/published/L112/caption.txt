Two traders enter the same setup at the same price. One sizes from the account balance, and the other sizes from the profit he made an hour ago.

The first position risks what the account can lose. The second risks what the last win felt like. A profit is not capital until it survives a drawdown, because unrealized gains disappear at the same speed they arrived.

Size from the balance, never from the last trade.
