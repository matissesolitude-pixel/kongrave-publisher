Two traders place an order. One picks a round number, the other picks a level the chart actually made — and only one of them is standing in a reservoir.

The round figure wasn't chosen by price. It was chosen by a habit, shared by thousands of people with the same habit, so orders and stops pile in the same place. Price runs the crowded side first: not to hunt anyone, simply because that's where there's enough resting volume to fill.
