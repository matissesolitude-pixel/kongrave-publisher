Two traders are wrong on the same trade. One adds a second entry to improve his average, and the other closes.

Adding looks free, because the average entry price improves the moment the second ticket goes in. But the risk did not improve: the position is now twice the size, on an idea the market has already refused once. The distance the price has to travel to hurt him has just been cut in half, at the exact moment his conviction was highest.

Improving your average is not the same as improving your trade.
