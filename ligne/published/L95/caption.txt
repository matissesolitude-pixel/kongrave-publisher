Two traders trade the same range for a week. One stops when the range breaks, and the other keeps selling the top.

For five days the market does the same thing and selling the top pays every time, so the habit is built on five real proofs. Then one move goes through the top and closes above it. Visually the difference is tiny — a body instead of a wick — and structurally it is a different market. The second trader is not wrong about the level. He is wrong about which game is being played around it.

A setup that stopped working looks exactly like one that still does.
