Every decision you make needs a second signature — and that signature is never yours.

The read is done, the level marked, the risk set. Nothing is missing except permission. So the decision goes out to be countersigned: a group, a mentor, a screenshot posted to see who agrees. By the time it comes back the price has moved exactly as far as the delay lasted, and both remaining options are worse than the one you prepared.

THE LINE — a decision that needs approval isn't slower, it's a different decision.
