Two traders take the exact same loss. One is trading normally an hour later. The other isn't back for three days.

It isn't the size — both were inside the limit. It isn't the setup either: same entry, same invalidation, both stopped out exactly as planned. One took the loss alone. The other took it in front of people, and now there's a second loss to recover from. It has no amount, no limit and no stop, because nothing was built to measure it.

THE LINE — a private loss costs one thing. A public one costs two.
